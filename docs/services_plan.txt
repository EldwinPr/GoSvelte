# GoSvelte Money Tracker - Service Architecture Plan

This document outlines the planned services and their core functions.

## 1. AuthService
Manages tenancy isolation and user access.
- Register: Creates a new Tenant and the first User.
- Login: Authenticates users and provides session context.
- GetContext: Retrieves user profile and TenantID.

## 2. AccountService
Manages financial accounts (Bank, Cash, etc.).
- CreateAccount: Initializes a new account for a tenant.
- GetDashboardSummary: Calculates net worth for a tenant.
- Reconcile: Audits stored balance against SUM(transactions).

## 3. TransactionService
The core ledger engine.
- RecordTransaction: Creates Income/Expense and updates balance atomically.
- UpdateTransaction: Adjusts balance based on delta changes.
- DeleteTransaction: Reverts balance impact and soft-deletes record.
- GetLedger: Returns paginated, filtered transaction lists.

## 4. TransferService
Handles movement between accounts.
- ExecuteTransfer: Atomic withdraw/deposit/fee logic.
- ReverseTransfer: Rolls back account balances and fees.

## 5. DebtService
Manages liabilities and lending.
- CreateDebt: Records new Lent or Borrowed funds.
- AddInstallment: Records payment, updates account, and reduces debt balance.
- GetDebtStatus: Summarizes active debts and due dates.

## 6. RecurringPaymentService
Automation for repeating entries.
- CreateSchedule: Sets up templates (e.g., Monthly Rent).
- ProcessPending: Generates transactions for due schedules.
- ToggleSchedule: Activates or deactivates automation.

## 7. BudgetService
Manages spending limits and discipline.
- SetBudget: Defines a spending limit for a specific Category or a Global limit.
- GetPerformance: Returns actual vs. budgeted amounts for the current period.
- CheckLimit: Internal helper to warn if a new transaction exceeds the budget.

## 8. WishlistService
Manages aspirational items (things to buy).
- CreateItem: Adds a new item with 1-6 scale ratings for Need, Want, and Productivity.
- UpdateProgress: Manually adjusts the amount "saved" or allocated to this item.
- GetRankedWishlist: 
    1. Fetches all active items for a tenant.
    2. Retrieves current Total Net Worth.
    3. Calculates "Financial Impact Penalty" for each item (Price vs. Balance).
    4. Generates a "Smart Score" and sorts the list by feasibility and impact.
- MarkPurchased: Handles final purchase logic (triggers a Transaction and archives the item).

## 9. SavingsGoalService
Manages long-term financial milestones (e.g., Emergency Fund).
- CreateGoal: Sets a target amount, date, and optional dedicated account.
- AllocateFunds: Transfers balance (virtually) toward the goal.
- GetProgress: Calculates percentage completion and projected completion date.

## 10. ReportingService
Insights and analytics.
- GetSpendingBreakdown: Groups expenses by Category.
- GetCashFlow: Monthly Income vs. Expense comparison.
- GetNetWorthHistory: Tracks the growth of total balance over time.
